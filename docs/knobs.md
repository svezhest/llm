# Env knobs

Current conclusions (the state of all topics: [MAP.md](MAP.md)):

- The complete table of environment knobs, worker and gateway, with defaults and the
  measurements behind them. A new knob gets its row here.
- Explicit knobs are A/B tools; the defaults are the measured best for the 9B. Per-model
  recipes (27B, 35B) are in [models.md](models.md) and the topic files.
